## Verdict

**Ship: 1.8.0 can be tagged from this nightly (`abcba70f1`). There are no blockers.** Both of last run's blockers are fixed and proven live on the boxes they were found on. The rollback now crosses the #3870 boundary into a pre-#3870 tree cleanly, and a fresh install's first Hermes provision stamps the real 65536 window. The update path is signed and clean. The only new defect is a minor `doctor perms` false positive on upgraded boxes.

**This verdict depends on its coverage, and the coverage was narrow.** Only 3 of 13 lanes ran on this build. The full lane sweep last ran on `…20261120045628`, which is two product commits back. Those two commits change 52 lines in `updater.py` and `hermes_provision.py`, and both paths were exercised directly here: the rollback, the fresh first provision, and every probe that touches either file. That is why a targeted run can support a ship verdict. It does **not** re-check anything the other ten lanes would have caught that the 160 probe results do not cover. Other coverage limits also apply: no fresh install with `/dev/kfd`, no lanes on prod, no ct163, and model-class probes on `lfm2.5-2.6b` only.

**Release-note items (known, not blockers):**
- **`hal0 doctor perms` exits 1 on most upgraded boxes**, with two DRIFT rows on `state/agents/hermes` (`0755, want 2755`). It is a false positive, and one `sudo hal0 doctor perms --fix` clears it for good. This is the new minor below. It reproduces on ct150 and on prod ct105, but not on a fresh install.
- **First-provision Hermes smoke ends `warn` on a fresh install** (#2671, open). The Agents card reads `degraded: provision_warn`, shown as amber "ready" on the Overview (by design per #3808 option b), until the next reprovision.
- **#2734: memory work serialises with chat on a fresh install** (`--parallel 1` brain). A Hermes MCP turn took 3 m 25 s on the contended brain this run. Workaround: bind a model to the memory slot.
- **Root-owned, byte-identical Hermes plugin files survive an upgrade** on boxes where an old root-run installer created them. This is harmless and by design: they are replaced by hal0-owned copies the first time the plugin content changes (verified live).
- `embed`/`rerank`/`tts` `type="llm"` residue on upgraded boxes needs `hal0 doctor profiles --fix` (#2693/#2729).
