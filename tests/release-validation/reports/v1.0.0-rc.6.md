**Method:** the full kit-driven pipeline, two boxes in parallel.

## Verdict

**Do not ship 1.0.0-rc.6 as GA — the Vulkan execution path in the release-pinned runner image produces invalid tokens for every model it serves, hal0's own documentation recommends that path, hal0's own benchmark lane pins it, and no layer anywhere between "container healthy" and "slot ready" validates that the box can produce language.**

The specific blockers:

* **GA-blocker — `brain-model-emits-garbage-tokens`.** `ghcr.io/hal0ai/hal0-rocmfpx:ade07ba` is a HIP+Vulkan single build. llama.cpp picks ROCm when `/dev/kfd` is visible and falls back to Vulkan when `ggml_rocm_init` fails; `providers/_gpu.resolve_gpu_device_paths` passes `/dev/kfd` only if it exists on the host. On ct151 it does not exist, so **every** slot ran the Vulkan lane and **every** text surface on the box was garbage while HTTP 200, container health, `hal0 doctor` "OK gpu", and 60–120 tok/s all read green. Proven decisively by an A/B on the *privileged, prod-class* ct150 with identical argv except `-dev`: `ROCm0` → *"Paris, a city that has been the center of French culture"*; `Vulkan0` → 12 tokens of nothing. Not model-specific (an unrelated Qwen3.5-0.8B Q4_K_XL is equally garbage on Vulkan), not contention (42 tok/s prompt / 123 tok/s eval in a standalone container), not privilege. Three release-owned consequences make this worse than an environment quirk: hal0 seeds and reports these slots as `device = "gpu-vulkan"` / `backend: vulkan` while on every box with `/dev/kfd` they actually execute on **ROCm** — so the lane hal0 advertises has never been exercised by any validation run; `docs/hal0-install-migration-guide.html` says *"RADV/Vulkan recommended on Strix Halo … v1.0 standardizes shared APU models on Vulkan"*; and `src/hal0/bench/harness.py:166` `vulkan_radv` pins `-dev Vulkan0`, so hal0's own benchmark lane measures throughput on invalid output. Scope, stated honestly: a bare-metal AMD install with amdkfd loaded silently lands on ROCm and is unaffected — the hit population is container/LXC deployments without `/dev/kfd`, hosts without amdkfd, and anyone who follows the documented Vulkan recommendation.
* **Major, and the reason to gate on it anyway — no output-sanity gate exists.** Slot `ready`, `/api/health`, `hal0 doctor`, the provision smoke, and the SSE `done` frame were all green on a box producing zero usable language for two hours. `hermes -z` hung forever; the install-time `chat_completions` smoke recorded "timed out"; hindsight fact extraction burned the GPU for 96 minutes. Every one of those was reported by a lane as a distinct defect, and every one of them is this. A `'The capital of France is'` → `'Paris'` probe at the end of slot load would have caught it in one second.
* **Major — `memory-extraction-never-lands` (#1834), and its CPU-only scoping is now disproved.** The retain queue provably does not drain on a box measured at **2716 tok/s prefill**. Three independent, hardware-agnostic defects in the pipeline turn *any* non-terminating or slow upstream into permanent slot saturation: no `max_tokens` cap on the extraction call (`n_predict=64000` against `n_ctx=65536`), no cancellation of the generation when hindsight's own 300 s client timeout fires (abandoned `id_task`s kept burning the GPU ~25 min past their deadline), and a ladder that **requeues after `attempt=4/4`** with `hindsight-api` `NRestarts=0` — an op created at 19:11:48 was still `processing` at `retry_count=1` after 96 minutes while ops created 40 minutes later reached terminal `failed`. Production (fact_count 10,354, 30 completed / 10 failed, terminal at rc=3) shows the ladder *does* bound on a healthy box — the requeue path appears to need a timeout rather than an error to trigger, which is consistent with the abandoned-request theory.
* **Major — `fpx-quant-guard-never-fires`.** `registry/pull.py::_register_pulled` builds `Model(...)` with no `quant=`, so every pull-registered model — including the installer-seeded brain model on **both** boxes — carries `quant = None`. Only the API list serializer backfills it (`lazy_quant` from `hf_filename`), so `GET /api/models` shows `"quant": "ROCmFP8"` and every user-facing surface looks right, while `SlotManager._resolve_model_info` hands `_guard_fpx_quant_runner` a `quant=None` dict and the guard returns at its first line. Reproduced end to end: `--hardware cpu` + the FPX brain model → **no 422**, container launched, `NRestarts=5`, `ExecMainStatus=1`. The #1790 belt-and-suspenders backstop is silently inert for every model hal0 installs itself. Every test in `TestFPXQuantRunnerGuard` hand-builds `_model_info(quant="ROCmFP8")`, which is why it shipped.

Everything else is major-and-below and would not, on its own, hold GA.

### What held up well

Thirteen of the twenty-five register entries came back **fixed** under adversarial probing, several of them rc.5 blockers, and the surfaces that were genuinely exercised held:

* **The rc.5 GA-blocker's mechanism is fixed.** `hermes-cannot-chat-64k-floor` (#1827): `/v1/models` advertises `context_length=65536` ≥ the 64,000 floor, the brain slot serves `--ctx-size 65536`, `GET /v1/models/hal0/agent` → 200, and the **new anchor preflight from #1877 ran and correctly passed** — *"anchor hal0/agent (slot brain) resolves to 65,536 tokens >= Hermes' 64,000 floor"*. The residual hermes hang is the garbage-token defect wearing a hermes costume, not the floor.
* **Context reporting is fully consistent — both #1788 and #1835 closed.** All four surfaces agree for the serving slot (`/api/slots`, `/api/slots/brain`, `resolved_command`, `/v1/models`, `systemctl cat`), *and* it was discriminated on a slot with a real ceiling/effective mismatch: a throwaway slot with TOML `context_size=4096` that resolved to `--ctx-size 512` reported **512 on every surface**. A register entry that survived two releases is dead.
* **`slot create` and the slot verbs are fixed.** #1830: `--profile` exists and is derived from `--type` — a `--type embedding` slot wrote `profile="embedding"`, carried `--embedding` in argv, loaded to ready, and returned a 768-dim vector from `/v1/embeddings`. #1832: no 10 s client cap remains — a failing load blocked 3m01s and exited 1 with the *real server verdict*, a successful load exited 0, an unload exited 0 in 0.5 s.
* **Reporting honesty is fixed across the provisioning surface.** #1793: `smoke_tests` reports `status=warn` with `failure_count=2` and both failures recorded verbatim, `skipped=[]`. #1831: **both** chat probes ran on the shipped config, and the pinned-single-backend resolution mode that hid in rc.5 now resolves through the backend endpoint. #1828: all five bundled skills linked, `/etc/hal0/agent-skills` `hal0:hal0 2775`, `fallbacks=[]`.
* **`brain-board-tools-401` (#1829) fixed the right way.** `brain/chat.py` rewires the board READ tools onto the local `BoardStore` via `hal0.board.store.resolve_store` — the same store `/api/board/*` uses — so the steward and the UI now read one board. Patching the token would have produced two.
* **The rc.5 UI rollup (#1841) is one-for-three, not clean** — item 2 (no CSS class leaking into `title`/`aria-label`) is fixed; items 1 (GPU/NPU telemetry gate) and 3 (unnamed rail-mode anchors) are not, and are tracked as `ui-slots-gpu-telemetry-host-leak` and `ui-openwebui-link-no-accessible-name`. #1836 (Overview Services widget) and #1837 (`/v1/models` never evicts) both fixed and verified after real churn.
* **`cli-api-polish-rollup` (#1796) fixed on all eight checked items** — `update --check`, system-info GPU row, `doctor` rc=0, `slot metrics` table, `bench results`, MCP status, typed 404, reranker classification under its upstream filename.
* **The update lane is the healthiest surface in the run: `post-upgrade` at 13/18 with one finding.** The rc.5 → rc.6 upgrade converged correctly, `restarted: null` + `restart_error` on a signal-killed self-restart is the *fix* for #1691 behaving as designed, and no operator surface reports the successful upgrade as failed. Routing at 10/13 with two minor findings; the dispatcher itself was correct in every probe.
* **The kit compounded, hard.** rc.5 ran 6 of 11 register entries and lost the entire mechanical batch to a quota error; rc.6 ran **26 of 26**. Adversarial verification killed or downgraded **8 of 35** candidates (23%), including four that would have sent a fix agent to revert a working rc.6 fix, and — the highest-value result of the run — it took a lane finding of *"brain model emits garbage, probably the model or the LXC"* and turned it into a reproducible, box-independent A/B on the runner image's Vulkan backend, which reframed three other lanes' findings as confounded rather than independent.

* **`api` and `cli` on `prod`.** `kit.toml` lists both for `boxes = ["fresh", "prod"]`; only `fresh` ran, for the second release running. Prod was used read-only for 19 targeted verification cross-checks instead — and it is still on **rc.5**, so it cannot validate rc.6 behaviour, only rule out box-specificity of pre-existing defects.
* **The `upgrade` lane could not exercise its primary subject.** The rc.5 → rc.6 upgrade was applied at 15:13 EDT, minutes before preflight reached the box; the lane audited a completed upgrade from the journal. In-flight upgrade behaviour (progress reporting, failure/rollback paths, the `prepare` → `commit` sequence as observed by a client) is **unverified**. Kit fix: the update box must be left on the from-version until its lane runs.
* **`fact-extraction-strips-literals` (#1794) is `blocked` for the second consecutive release**, and for the same reason both times — no retain ever reached `completed`. The rc.5 kit fix (chain the probe behind a completed retain) was applied and is not the problem; #1834 is. It will block again next release unless #1834 is fixed first.
* **`brain-tools-image-gate` (#1789) could not be positively confirmed for the second consecutive release.** The gate is verified fixed in source and config; the "tool rounds engage" clause is unobservable while the box cannot generate.
* **No test models were staged.** `/mnt/ai-models` on ct151 held no ggufs, so lanes pulled or fabricated their own mid-run. This directly cost coverage: the rerank half of `profile-flags-argv` (#1787) could not be demonstrated, and the only reranker available was the broken catalog row. Stage the models before the run, as rc.5 did.
* **Concurrency contaminated the slots lane.** A second actor created `zz*`/`zzfpx`/`zzcrash`/`zzrc6mech` slots on ct151 during the slots lane window; capacity, port and failed-unit assertions after 19:22 UTC are unreliable and that residue must not be attributed to the lane. Several verification agents also worked the box simultaneously — no wall-clock timing from the 20:40–21:10 window is trustworthy.

**What the fleet cannot test at all**

* **There is still no representative GPU fresh-install box — and this run proves the gap is worse than "we lack GPU coverage".** ct151 *has* a render node and NPU passthrough but **no `/dev/kfd`**, so it exercises the Vulkan lane exclusively. ct150 and prod *have* `/dev/kfd`, so they silently execute on ROCm while their slot TOMLs say `gpu-vulkan`. **Neither configuration matches a typical bare-metal AMD install, and until this run nobody knew which lane any box was actually running.** That is precisely how a totally broken Vulkan backend survived to rc.6. Every future preflight must record which llama.cpp device the box's slots actually select, not which one the TOML declares.
* **Production is on rc.5.** Every "prod is fine" statement in this report means "prod on the previous release, with months of operator tuning, escapes by history" — the same trap that hid `brain-get-board-401` and `bundled-skills-not-linked` through rc.5.
* **ct150 has no snapshot** and its value is its upgrade history, so update-lane mutations are permanent and were kept minimal.
* **Timings are not product timings.** ct151 is 8 cores / 16 GiB with an iGPU, frequently under 5–10 concurrent agents.

## Kit changes proposed

Applied by PR, not silently.

**New `known-issues.yaml` entries** — all eight reclassifications above, each with the drafted `why:` and `still_report_if:`. Highest value, because each prevents a rediscovery. Additionally, from the verification pass:

* `chat-to-embed-slot-500-passthrough` (by-design) — Rule 7 name pinning + verbatim upstream non-2xx pass-through compose; the registry type of the named model is irrelevant, and with `embed` offline the same request returns a clean 404.
* `capability-apply-persists-intent-on-lifecycle-failure` (by-design, #697) — capabilities.toml is a desired-state file committed as one atomic ChangeSet before lifecycle dispatch; five surfaces report the failure truthfully. Only the missing CLI `Status` column is the defect.
* `cli-slot-show-panel-clips-long-json-lines` (by-design) — Rich `Syntax(word_wrap=False)` clipping at terminal width, not a slice; `--json` is documented on the command and emits the full payload. Same adjudication as `cli-agent-status-detail-truncated`.
* `doctor-perms-secrets-dir-0755-is-declared` (by-design, partial) — the wanted mode *is* 0755 with `child_mode=0600` on the `.env` glob; `--fix` exposes agent-id enumeration, not credential content. Do **not** re-file as "doctor leaks secrets", and do not assert a DRIFT row *count* (it grows with every slot loaded).
* `memory-extraction-quality-is-anchor-dependent` (by-design) — `HINDSIGHT_API_LLM_MODEL=hal0/utility` with a model-less seeded `utility` resolving to the `agent` anchor is ADR-0023 Rule 9 and is documented at length in `hindsight-api.service`. The context-preflight gap is the filable part.
* `ui-inference-card-header-is-device-highlight` (by-design) — `iGPU` deliberately aggregates vulkan+rocm; the FLM chip points at the cordoned NPU pane; the authoritative count is the footer's "slots N", which is correct.
* `cli-api-polish-rollup-1840-still-open` (process) — a validation run reports #1840 per-item against the register and ticks checkboxes; it does not re-file the aggregate, and it does not aggregate 13 items into a severity above the highest individual item.

**Lane brief and `_shared.md` additions**

* **`_shared.md`, first: an output-sanity precondition.** Before any lane reports a finding that depends on generated text, run `POST <slot-port>/completion {"prompt":"The capital of France is","n_predict":12,"temperature":0}` and confirm the answer contains "Paris". If it does not, every text-dependent check in that lane is `blocked`, and the finding is against the runner, not the feature. This one paragraph would have collapsed four of this run's lane reports into one.
